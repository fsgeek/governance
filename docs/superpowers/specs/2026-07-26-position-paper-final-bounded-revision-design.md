# Final Bounded Position-Paper Revision Design

**Date:** July 26, 2026

**Objective:** Resolve the small set of genuine boundary defects identified in
the second review round without turning *Architectures of Absence* into an
implementation standard, examination manual, or proposal for new legal rights.

## Revision Boundary

The final pass makes five coordinated repairs.

1. **Representation, access, participation, and remedy.** State that these are
   distinct. The ontology gives affected interests standing in analysis; it
   does not itself grant artifact access, participation rights, burden shifting,
   or a remedy. Identify existing or institutionally available contest pathways
   without inventing mandates, and state where representation remains indirect.

2. **Immediate and collective action.** Move the theory of effect forward. One
   bank can inventory evidence dependencies, narrow unsupported claims, use
   proportionate compensating processes, record residual limitations, and make
   procurement needs comparable. Cross-vendor bindings, standard exports,
   convergent supervisory expectations, and market leverage require collective
   infrastructure. Neither pathway is represented as guaranteed.

3. **Adjacent theory.** Position the artifact-to-inference structure against
   assurance-case reasoning and position friction-dependent observability
   against administrative-burden and related selection literature. The paper's
   contribution remains their synthesis with affected-party absence and exact
   FS AI RMF control applications, not invention of claims, evidence, warrants,
   missing data, or administrative burden.

4. **Legal and authority precision.** Verify the pricing discussion against
   primary sources concerning FCRA risk-based-pricing notices, credit-score
   disclosures, and Regulation B adverse-action/counteroffer boundaries. Audit
   every mandatory verb so an FS AI RMF control objective, adopted internal
   policy, supervisory expectation, and legal requirement remain distinct.

5. **Dual-register terminology.** Retain *silence-manufacture* as the critical
   name for the recurring pattern. Use *evidentiary-substitution sequence* as
   its neutral operational description. State that neither term establishes
   intention, causation, violation, or legal significance.

## Explicit Exclusions

This revision will not add JSON or YAML schemas, APIs, JWT manifests, formal
policy languages, AI bills of materials, retention schedules, materiality
thresholds, mandatory burden shifting, ombudsmen, new contest rights, claims of
privilege, implementation appendices, or practitioner checklists. Those choices
require empirical, engineering, legal, or policy work beyond this paper.

The paper may acknowledge that diagnostic records can have litigation,
discovery, supervisory, confidentiality, and security consequences and that
institutions need context-specific legal advice. It will not present privilege
as guaranteed or as a method for concealing known risk.

## Completion Gates

The revision is ready for author review only when:

- affected-party centrality no longer implies participation or remedy;
- immediate and collective theories of effect are visible near the opening;
- assurance-case and administrative-burden antecedents are accurately sourced;
- the pricing and authority language survives primary-source checking;
- critical and operational terminology are both defined and used consistently;
- second-round findings outside scope are not silently imported;
- source length remains controlled and the narrative remains regulator-readable;
- citations, references, labels, clean LuaLaTeX build, rendered PDF, independent
  arXiv package, and repository tests all pass;
- the final manuscript is committed, timestamped, and pushed before handoff.

The user's next substantive review is the arXiv-readiness review, not an
intermediate design or line-edit approval.
