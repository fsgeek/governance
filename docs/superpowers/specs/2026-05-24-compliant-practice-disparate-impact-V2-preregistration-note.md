## 0. The shift from V1 (why this experiment exists)

- **V1 (transform):** an intentional post-hoc transform routes a discriminatory model's signal onto admissible carriers to evade audit. Behavioral claim about transforms; requires intent.
- **V2 (compliant practice):** a model trained on admissible features with the protected attribute G and the policy-declared proxies excluded — **standard ML, no transform anywhere** — already reproduces disparate impact, because real features are not independent and admissible carriers ambiently hold G's signal. Structural claim about audit doctrine; **requires no intent.**

V2 is the regulator's nightmare: not "predatory actors can evade audit" but "competent practitioners produce audit-evading disparate-impact models without trying, and the audit machinery cannot determine whether the carrier is an illegitimate proxy or a legitimate predictor." V1 does **not** imply V2 — one cannot prove *intent-free* discrimination by exhibiting an *intentful* transform — so this is a separate experiment, not a corollary.

**The training target Y (pinned).** `Y` is the **contaminated decision label**: `Y ~ Bernoulli(σ(legit_logit + disp))`, where `legit_logit = X·β_legit` is legitimate underwriting signal and `disp` is the channel's injected prohibited-policy term (`dgp.py:157-160`). `M_full` and `M_compliant` train on `Y`. `M_oracle` trains on the **clean target** `Y_clean ~ Bernoulli(σ(legit_logit))` (the disparate term zeroed; substrate amendment Item 2) and defines the legitimate-disparity baseline. **V2 therefore models compliant training on historically contaminated decisions, not on a purely legitimate outcome** — this is the realistic case (lenders train on past approve/deny labels produced under the prior policy) and it is what makes admissible carriers reconstruct the prohibited signal.

**The protected attribute `G` is LATENT to the audit (pinned, rev-5).** No model the audit inspects, and no reference R1–R6 (§2e) except the explicit BISG-regulator reference R4, has access to `G`. `G` is observable only to the *experimenter*, who uses it to (a) construct the discriminatory upper baseline `M_full`, (b) measure disparate impact (`Δ`, §2b), and (c) define the dose axis `proxy_strength` (§2c). This mirrors real fair lending — race is not in the lender's model; a regulator may *estimate* it (BISG) but does not observe it — and, crucially, it is what makes C3 a genuine non-identifiability result rather than a missing-variable artifact (§2d).

## 1. Claims under test

- **C1 — compliant practice retains disparate impact, DOSE-DEPENDENTLY (headline).** `M_compliant` (standard ML on admissible features, G + `declared_proxies` excluded, no transform) reproduces a substantial fraction of the *excess* disparate impact of `M_full`, and that fraction **increases with the admissible-carrier↔G proxy strength** (the dose axis `proxy_strength`, §2c). The deliverable is the curve `retained_excess(proxy_strength)`, not a point estimate. **Reported alongside:** `external_carrier_lift` (§2b) — how much of the retention is the *external* carrier `c_fresh` versus the named features already reconstructing G.
- **C2 — compliant practice passes the behavioral + set-structural rungs.** `M_compliant` passes rung 1 (SHAP/LIME: no declared-proxy in attributions), rung 2 (disagreement), and rung 3a (set reorganization), because its impact rides admissible carriers the audit treats as legitimate.
- **C3 — the enumeration / non-identifiability limit (the payload).** Stated as TWO objects so the falsifiable content is a measured quantity, not a re-derivation of a known theorem. **Both rest on `G` being latent to the audit (§0):**
  - **C3-floor (formal, cited not claimed).** With `G` latent, whether `c_fresh`'s association with `Y` reflects *illegitimate proxy use* (`c_fresh ← G → Y`: a non-causal marker of the latent protected attribute, which drives the contaminated label discriminatorily) or *legitimate prediction* (`c_fresh → Y`: a genuine cause of the outcome) is **not identified by the observable joint distribution** — the canonical confounding-vs-causation non-identifiability (Pearl's do-calculus: `c_fresh ← G → Y` and `c_fresh → Y` are observationally equivalent absent intervention or observation of the latent `G`; Spirtes-Glymour-Scheines; Kilbertus et al.). We **instantiate** this in the lending-audit setting via the twin-world construction (§2d), matched on the *observable* (`G`-free) joint, as a worked *illustration*, not a discovery. **We learned the hard way that the construction MUST hold `G` latent:** with `G` observable, the two worlds differ in conditional-independence structure and a discriminator separates them trivially — that is missing-variable detection, not non-identifiability. The contribution of C3-floor is the lending-audit instantiation and its scoping (§5), not the theorem.
  - **C3-payload (empirical, the bet).** On the realistic substrate where the *experimenter* knows the ground-truth proxy/legitimate label (World A vs World B), measure how well the **pre-specified family of data-derived references** (R1–R6, §2e) — operating *without* `G`, except the one explicit BISG-regulator reference R4 — separates proxy-use from legitimate-use of `c_fresh`, **as a function of `proxy_strength`**. The deliverable is the curve `separation_auc(proxy_strength)` per reference, plus the **information-set contrast** (does the BISG-`G` reference beat the `G`-free ones?). A reference that achieves useful separation is a deployable risk-triage even though C3-floor proves it cannot *certify* legitimacy. Separation is not assumed zero; the curve is the surprising, falsifiable object — and rev-5 **asserts no particular shape** (P-C3-payload).
- **C4 — the optimized upper bound (V2_adv) — SECONDARY, intent-requiring.** Searching over admissible feature selection/weighting to *maximize* excess disparate impact subject to passing the behavioral rungs (1/2/3a) and AUC ≥ AUC(`M_full`) − γ reaches near-`M_full` impact while leaving no footprint detectable under rungs 1/2/3a. **This is intentful by construction — it is the V1 `T_adv` analog with the transform indirection removed, an upper bound on adversarial admissible practice. It is NOT part of the intent-free V2 payload (C1–C3)**; it complements V1's intent-based framing. Reported as a bound, flagged as such.

## 2. Operational definitions

- **2a. Model family** (all predict the decision Y unless noted; AUC-comparable):
  - `M_full` — trained on all of X including `G` and `declared_proxies`. The **experimenter-side discriminatory upper baseline**: it uses the latent `G`, so only the experimenter can build it — it is not an audit-visible model; it bounds maximal disparate impact.
  - `M_compliant` — trained on `(V_named ∪ V_ext) \ declared_proxies` (= `x0..x5 ∪ c_fresh`). **No transform.** The headline test article.
  - `M_strict` — trained on `V_named` only (`x0..x5`). Strictest policy reading. **Differs from `M_compliant` by exactly the `c_fresh` portfolio**, which is what makes `external_carrier_lift` a clean isolation of `c_fresh`'s proxy contribution.
  - `M_oracle` — trained on `Y_clean` over the **world-appropriate legitimate feature set**, defining the **legitimate baseline** Δ (an experimenter-side construct; `Y_clean` and the world labels are latent to the audit). World-specific for the twin-world (§2d): in **World A** (`c_fresh ← G → Y`, `c_fresh` a non-causal proxy of latent `G`) `Y_clean_A = σ(legit_logit)` and the oracle uses `V_named` (excludes `c_fresh`); in **World B** (`c_fresh → Y`, genuine cause) `Y_clean_B = σ(legit_logit + θ_B·c_fresh)` and the oracle uses `V_named ∪ c_fresh`. The reported `M_oracle` baseline for the realistic substrate is the World-A oracle. This keeps the World-B oracle from treating a legitimate carrier as illegitimate (which would make World B "legitimate" in name only).
  - **AUC-comparability caveat (pinned) — promoted to a MANDATORY reported figure.** `M_compliant` drops `x6,x7`, which carry *legitimate* signal (β=0.60, 0.35). Its AUC deficit vs `M_full` is therefore part discrimination-avoidance, part legitimate-signal loss. The γ budget in the rung gate (§2f) absorbs the legitimate-signal cost of the prohibition, not only the discrimination cost. **The result note MUST plot the decomposition as a primary figure: `AUC(M_full) − AUC(M_strict)` labelled "legitimate signal lost to the prohibition" vs `AUC(M_full) − AUC(M_compliant)` labelled "additional loss under compliant practice."** This forecloses the "fairness costs too much" misread (the prohibition removing legitimate features ≠ the disparate-impact finding) that a policy audience would otherwise make.
- **2b. Disparate impact, measured as EXCESS over the legitimate baseline, sign-anchored.** `Δ(M) ≡ P(Ŷ=1|G=0) − P(Ŷ=1|G=1)`. Define `s ≡ sign(Δ(M_full) − Δ(M_oracle))` (fixed per channel/seed) so positive means worse-for-the-protected-group, and `adverse_excess(M) ≡ s·(Δ(M) − Δ(M_oracle))`. Because legitimate risk is entangled with G (G is generated from `x0`), `Δ(M_oracle)` is nonzero and can be sign-reversed, so raw `Δ` is not the discrimination measure. **`retained_excess ≡ adverse_excess(M_compliant) / adverse_excess(M_full)`**, reported signed; **a run is valid for `retained_excess` only if `adverse_excess(M_full) ≥ ε_excess` (§2h)** to keep the denominator away from zero. **`external_carrier_lift ≡ adverse_excess(M_compliant) − adverse_excess(M_strict)`** — the marginal contribution of the `c_fresh` portfolio; interpreted as *proxy lift* in World-A / proxy-labelled channels and as *legitimate external-carrier lift* in World B (the statistic is world-neutral; only its interpretation is world-conditional). `lift_ratio ≡ external_carrier_lift / adverse_excess(M_full)`.
- **2c. The dose axis `proxy_strength` (V2-specific substrate addition).** `proxy_strength ≡ AUC(Ĝ)` of a frozen classifier predicting G from the `c_fresh` portfolio alone — a single metric that spans the portfolio's three distributional families (continuous, sparse-categorical, heavy-tailed count), where Pearson ρ would not. The DGP exposes a **frozen coupling knob** calibrated to hit each target `proxy_strength` on the grid (§2h). `proxy_strength` is the independent variable; C1 and C3-payload are statements about curves over it. (This subsumes and promotes the proxy-strength sweep the substrate amendment flagged as optional; it is core to V2.) **Classifier pinned (no hidden moving part):** L2-logistic on the standardized 3-member portfolio, **out-of-sample 5-fold cross-fitted AUC on the calibration split only** (never the train/test used for the models). Report the portfolio AUC (the dose value) AND the per-member univariate AUCs for `cfresh_cont`/`cfresh_cat`/`cfresh_count`, so the dose axis is auditable. (`proxy_strength` is an **experimenter-side** substrate parameter — it uses `G`, which the audit does not see; it labels the dose, it is not a quantity the audit computes.)
- **2d. Twin-world substrate (for C3-floor) — `G`-latent confounding vs causation.** At a fixed `proxy_strength`, generate two worlds that share the same *observable* (`G`-free) joint `P(V_named, c_fresh, Y)` but differ in the latent causal status of `c_fresh`:
  - **World A (proxy):** `c_fresh ← G → Y` — latent `G` drives both `c_fresh` and the contaminated label discriminatorily; `c_fresh` is a non-causal marker of latent `G`.
  - **World B (legitimate):** `c_fresh → Y` — `c_fresh` is a genuine cause of the outcome; calibrated (`θ_B`) so the worlds match on the observable joint.

  Because `G` is latent (the audit never sees it), the two are observationally non-identifiable — the canonical confounding-vs-causation result. **Calibration target (pinned):** a flexible discriminator trained to classify World-A vs World-B from all *audit-observable* data — `(V_named, c_fresh, Y, model outputs)`, **NOT `G`** — achieves accuracy ≤ `0.5 + ε_world` (§2h) on the **full observable joint**. **"Model outputs" here means only outputs available under the same audit information set** — `M_compliant`/`M_strict` and the `G`-free references; it explicitly **excludes** `M_full`, `M_oracle`, world labels, true-`G`, and any model trained using true `G` (those are experimenter-side and would leak the answer). The match must hold on the full joint, not a single marginal: a battery of conditional-summary checks (`P(Y | c_fresh, V_named)`, calibration-by-bin, conditional-permutation behavior, and the higher-order moments a GBT discriminator can exploit) lies within tolerance. The DGP-aware **oracle** confirms the worlds are genuinely causally distinct (not a degenerate match) via the **conditional-dependence test `Y ⊥ G | observables`**: World A *violates* it (adding `G` to an observables-only `Y`-model lifts out-of-sample AUC by a margin — `G` drives `Y` beyond observables), World B *satisfies* it (`G`-lift ≈ 0, since `Y` was drawn from `p_obs(observables)`). The oracle separation is this conditional-independence contrast, **NOT** a per-row world classifier — "`Y` depends on `G` beyond observables" vs "`Y ⊥ G | observables`" is a distributional property, invisible row-by-row (a build smoke confirmed a per-row classifier with `G` sits at chance ≈ 0.57; the `G`-lift contrast is the right oracle, clearly positive in A and ≈0 in B). **This is what "matched" must mean — the rev-4→rev-5 lesson, learned from a build diagnostic:** matching only `corr(c_fresh,Y)` while leaving other observable relations (e.g. `G→Y`, hence `P(Y|c_fresh,V_named)`) free does NOT match the joint, and a discriminator exploits the residual difference; calibration MUST drive the *full-joint* without-`G` discriminator to chance. `θ_B` is calibration-determined (set so the full observable joint matches), not a free constant. If the discriminator exceeds `0.5 + ε_world`, the worlds leaked observable structure and the calibration is rejected (substrate bug, fixed pre-freeze). **Per-grid-point + soft-pass (pinned):** calibration is attempted at *every* grid point and adjudicated per passing point (the set of passing points is itself a finding about where the distinction is observationally reachable); a discriminator in `(0.53, 0.56]` with oracle ≥ 0.90 is logged "imperfectly-matched-but-structurally-valid"; only `> 0.56` (or oracle `< 0.90`) is a true failure.
- **2e. Audit rungs + the data-derived 3b reference FAMILY (load-bearing).** Rungs identical to the amended V1 instruments (rung 1 KernelSHAP+LIME; rung 2 surrogate-band disagreement; rung 3a `classify_reorganized` with `prohibited = {G} ∪ declared_proxies`; rung 3b importance-reference). **For C3, rung 3b is run against a pre-specified family of best-effort data-derived references**, each producing a *separation score* (its AUC for ranking known-proxy use above known-legitimate use). The references take only **audit-observable inputs** (`V_named`, `c_fresh`, `Y`, model outputs; never the latent `G` — except R4, which uses the BISG estimate `Ĝ_BISG`). The ground-truth proxy/legitimate labels used to *score* `separation_auc` are experimenter-side (World A vs World B), for evaluation only:
  - **R1 — held-out same-pool importance** (`c_fresh` importance in a model on the same admissible pool, held-out fold). *The deliberately weak/circular reference, retained as the null floor so the family exhibits a gradient — NOT the operative test.*
  - **R2 — conditional permutation importance** of `c_fresh` controlling for `V_named`: conditional-permutation scheme (permute `c_fresh` within `V_named`-defined strata via the fitted GBT), 20 repeats, importance = mean AUC drop.
  - **R3 — residualized-carrier importance**: residualize each `c_fresh` member on `V_named` with a train-fold model matched to its family (GBT-regression for `cont`, logistic for `cat`, Poisson for `count`), apply train-derived models to the test fold, then importance of the residuals in the GBT.
  - **R4 — the explicit BISG-regulator reference (the one `G`-using member, flagged).** `I(c_fresh; Y | V_named)` (`G`-free) vs `I(c_fresh; Ĝ_BISG | V_named)`, where `Ĝ_BISG` is a *regulator-estimated* protected attribute (the BISG / geocoded proxy standard in fair lending — NOT the latent true `G`). Kraskov–Stögbauer–Grassberger kNN CMI estimator (KSG variant 1, k=5; `npeet` impl pinned — CMI estimates diverge by implementation). R4 represents the stronger *regulator* information set; its contrast with the `G`-free references (R1/R2/R3/R5/R6) is the reported with-/without-protected-estimate comparison (§3 P-C3-payload-IS).
  - **R5 — double-ML, the strongest adversary**: DML with GBT nuisances (outcome `Y ~ V_named`, treatment `c_fresh ~ V_named`), 5-fold cross-fitting; causal-forest (EconML) as a robustness arm. Effect = partialled-out `c_fresh→Y`. *The reference most likely to achieve partial separation; if it does, that is the deployable-triage finding.*
  - **R6 — reliance-shift (explicit, oracle-free):** `R6 ≡ adverse_reliance − legit_benchmark`, where `adverse_reliance` is the `G`-adverse-direction component of `E[Ŷ_compliant] − E[Ŷ_strict]` and `legit_benchmark` is `c_fresh`'s `V_named`-residual predictive value for `Y` (audit-estimable, **no oracle**: the cross-fitted lift of `c_fresh` for `Y` after partialling `V_named`). So `R6` is adverse reliance beyond what `c_fresh`'s observable residual predictiveness justifies. *(rev-5 dropped R6's earlier `M_oracle_B`−`M_oracle_A` term — an auditor has no oracle; the adverse-direction sign uses the experimenter's `s` only for scoring, not as an audit input.)* Its `separation_auc` ranks proxy channels above legitimate ones. *(Estimator knobs for R2–R5 are pinned at the consequential grain; ancillary settings inherit library defaults, frozen in the committed harness.)*
  - **Adjudication (pinned).** Each reference's separation score is its `separation_auc`. **C3-floor "holds as illustrated"** at a grid point iff the without-`G` calibration discriminator (§2d) is ≤ `0.5 + ε_world` there (the cited non-identifiability instantiated). **C3-payload** reports `separation_auc(proxy_strength)` per reference (the `G`-free references and the R4 BISG reference separately), the best-`G`-free-reference curve, and the `G`-free-vs-BISG contrast. A `G`-free reference clearing the `δ_sep` margin-over-null (§2h) in a useful regime is a deployable risk-triage (a C3-payload "win", a partial MISS against strong impossibility — the bigger, reported result). **Failed-calibration grid points (pinned, rev-6):** C3-payload separation is scored **only on grid points where C3-floor calibration passes** (§2d). Points that fail calibration are plotted separately as calibration failures / observable-leakage findings, **not** counted as evidence for or against any reference. If `proxy_strength* = 0.70` itself fails calibration, the P-C3-payload point-score is marked *unevaluable at the anchor* and the curve is interpreted over the calibrated points only.
- **2f. V2_adv search (C4).** CMA-ES over a weighting/subset of the admissible pool; objective = maximize `adverse_excess` s.t. rungs 1/2/3a pass and a **dual AUC gate**: the *full gate* `AUC(V2_adv) ≥ AUC(M_full) − γ` AND the *admissible gate* `AUC(V2_adv) ≥ AUC(best admissible baseline) − γ` (best admissible baseline = the better of `M_compliant`/`M_strict`). Reporting both lets a MISS distinguish "adversarial admissible practice failed" from "the full gate penalized the model for not using prohibited/declared-proxy information" (since `M_full` may use G + `declared_proxies`). Budget §2h. **What C4 adds beyond the V2 payload (its reason to exist):** if V2_adv reaches near-`M_full` impact, it establishes that V1's *transform indirection was not a necessary condition for adversarial evasion* — ordinary admissible feature engineering suffices. C4 thereby closes the loop between the structural claim (V2: happens without trying) and the adversarial claim (V1: can be optimized), showing the two are endpoints of one continuum rather than separate phenomena — and retiring V1's transform machinery as an unnecessary complication, not a different result.
- **2g. Frozen numerics & valid-run checklist.** Inherit the amended substrate scalars (`n` = 20k/5k/25k train/val/test, |X|=8, `declared_proxies = {x6,x7}`, AUC band, γ, control-qualification). Generator code committed at stamp is the binding spec.
- **2h. Pinned substrate constants (rev-2, updated rev-5 — these are the irreversible bets alongside §3 priors):**
  - **Audit information set (pinned, rev-5):** `G` is latent to the audit and to all references except R4 (which uses the BISG estimate `Ĝ_BISG`). `G` is experimenter-only (for `M_full`, `Δ`, `proxy_strength`). See §0/§2d.
  - **`Ĝ_BISG` construction (pinned, rev-6):** a simulated regulator estimate of `G`, since the synthetic has no surnames/geocoding. `Ĝ_BISG` is a continuous score formed from the **latent `G` plus calibrated noise ONLY** — its inputs are `G` and noise; it may **NOT** use `c_fresh` or any audit feature (that would be circular). Calibrated so out-of-sample `AUC(Ĝ_BISG ~ G) = 0.85` (the published BISG accuracy band), frozen once per seed, constant across the `proxy_strength` grid. **Swept** as sensitivity over `{0.75, 0.85, 0.95}` (weak / realistic / strong regulator estimate) — R4's advantage (P-C3-payload-IS) is reported as a function of BISG quality, since at AUC→1 R4 trivially deconfounds and at AUC→0.5 it cannot, so only the realistic band is informative.
  - **`proxy_strength` grid:** `{0.50, 0.55, 0.60, 0.65, 0.70, 0.75, 0.80, 0.85}` (AUC(G ~ c_fresh portfolio)), realized by calibrating the frozen coupling knob per target (±0.01 tolerance). The **`0.50` point is the null anchor** (pure-noise carrier, coupling knob = 0) — the zero-retention / zero-separation floor against which the rest of the curve is read.
  - **P-C1 bet point:** `proxy_strength* = 0.70` — **arbitrary-but-frozen, NOT a claim about real lending** (we have no domain calibration for it and assert none). It exists only to fix a falsifiable point for the §3 prior. The deliverable is the curve, not this point — see §2i.
  - **`ε_excess` (denominator validity floor):** `0.05`.
  - **`ε_world` (twin-world discriminator tolerance):** `0.03` — the **without-`G`** discriminator (audit-observable inputs only) on the **full observable joint** must be ≤ 0.53. The oracle check is the conditional-dependence contrast (§2d): `G`-lift ≥ `0.02` AUC in World A and `G`-lift(A) − `G`-lift(B) ≥ `0.02` (the worlds are causally distinct). Matching a single marginal is insufficient (§2d). **Build-verified (pre-stamp smoke):** the matched-joint construction calibrates on **8 of 8** grid points (without-`G` discriminator ≈ 0.50 throughout) — so the P-C3-floor 0.70 prior is conservative; calibration is near-analytic.
  - **`θ_sep` (consumer-facing deployability bar):** `0.60` — **arbitrary-but-frozen**, used only for the §2i consumer surface (the regime in which each reference clears θ across [0.55, 0.80]). **It is NOT the P-C3-payload bet** (rev-4 moved the bet off the absolute crossing to avoid a 0.59 cliff — see `δ_sep`).
  - **`δ_sep` (margin-over-null, the P-C3-payload bet metric):** `0.05`. The bet is that the best `G`-free reference's `separation_auc` exceeds the **R1-null** by ≥ `δ_sep` at **any C3-floor-calibrated grid point** (primary; the low-dose `≤ 0.65` version is a *secondary* subclaim, §3) — a continuous margin-over-null, not an absolute-threshold crossing, so a separation of 0.59-vs-R1-0.52 still counts as the real signal it is. **R1-null sanity check (pinned):** if R1's own `separation_auc` exceeds `0.55` averaged across calibrated grid points, R1 is not functioning as a null floor and the margin-over-null metric is reconsidered before result interpretation (reported, not silently dropped).
  - **`τ_launder` (control-qualification c_fresh excess-importance ceiling):** null-calibrated — the 95th percentile of `c_fresh` importance under `C_null` (clean target, no proxy use) bootstrap; **swept ±50%** as sensitivity (declare-and-sweep, [[project_knob_robustness_result]]).
  - **`γ` (AUC gate for V2_adv):** `0.02` AUC.
  - **V2_adv budget:** CMA-ES population 16, max 200 generations or convergence (Δobjective < 1e-3 over 20 generations), hard ceiling 5000 evaluations.
  - **`c_fresh`:** the 3-family portfolio `{cfresh_cont, cfresh_cat, cfresh_count}` (already in the DGP).
  - **Model class for the family:** GBT default; sparse-logistic + depth-≤4 CART as robustness arms (§4).
  - **Seed & uncertainty policy (pinned):** `N = 50` independent **paired** seeds per (`proxy_strength` × world × channel) for the primary GBT family; `N = 20` for the robustness arms (same seeds where computationally feasible). All rates and curves report **paired-seed bootstrap 95% intervals**. Rate predictions (e.g. "passes on ≥90% of seeds") and monotonicity are adjudicated against these intervals, never visually (see §4 for the monotonicity rule). `N = 50` is a feasibility-informed (not power-calculated) choice; the result note reports CI widths so any under-power for inverted-U detection is visible rather than hidden.
  - **Compute budget & calibration fallback (pinned):** the synthetic substrate is cheap relative to the FM/HMDA pipelines (`n = 50k` total per run, GBT fits) — full grid × worlds × `N=50` + CMA-ES is hours, not the multi-day FM band runs. **C3-floor reportability floor:** if twin-world calibration passes on `< 4 of 8` grid points, C3-floor is reported as **regime-limited** (the points where it holds, plus the leak characterization §3) — it is never silently abandoned, and a sub-floor result is a finding about observational reachability, not a null.

- **2i. ## 5. Scope / exclusions
- **Single-environment observational scope (the C3 scoping that keeps it defensible).** C3-floor is a **single-environment, iid, observational** non-identifiability claim: with no construction provenance, no intervention, no environment shift, no instruments, and no external semantic/causal knowledge, two worlds can share the observed joint distribution while differing in whether `c_fresh` is a proxy or a legitimate cause. The claim is **NOT** "no method in principle can separate proxy from legitimate use." Causal inference exists — interventional data, natural experiments, multi-jurisdiction/temporal environment shift, instruments, and provenance documentation can reach the distinction. The honest statement: *"No single-environment observational audit over the submitted model, audit-visible features, outcomes, and any audit-visible protected-class **estimate** (e.g. BISG) can decide legitimacy; separation requires observation of the **true** protected attribute, external causal/provenance information, intervention, environment shift, or substantive legal judgment."* This ties to [[reference_ai_honesty_paper]] (necessary-not-sufficient): the data-only audit is necessary but cannot be sufficient for the legitimacy verdict. **The non-identifiability here is specifically confounding-vs-causation with the protected attribute `G` latent (§0/§2d):** `c_fresh ← G → Y` (proxy) and `c_fresh → Y` (legitimate) are observationally equivalent when `G` is unobserved — which is the fair-lending reality (race absent from the lender's model). A regulator's BISG *estimate* of `G` partially deconfounds (R4) but does not restore identifiability.
- **"Audit-evading" — narrowed.** Compliant practice can evade **enumeration-based and data-only legitimacy-*certifying*** audits (declared-proxy enumeration, SHAP/LIME declared-proxy checks, set-reorganization, the data-derived 3b family). It does **not** necessarily evade a simple **proxy-risk *screen*** ("this important feature is highly predictive of G and the model carries large adverse excess → route to legal/substantive review"). Such a screen detects *risk* but cannot *decide legitimacy*. This is not a retreat — it is the sharper claim: noticing the correlation is easy; what observational audit cannot do is answer the legal question the correlation raises.
- **Static correlation.** `proxy_strength` is fixed per run. The real world adds that the correlation structure *evolves* (VRA/redistricting: precincts *became* race proxies) — enumeration doesn't fail once, it **decays**; a declared vocabulary is indexed to a correlation snapshot ([[project_pragmatics_linguistics_lens]] indexicality, time as context-of-utterance). One scope-out sentence here; a load-bearing line for the regulatory paper. The synthetic does not model dynamics.
- **NOT real-substrate** (HMDA + SBA deferred). **Measurement bridge (for the deferred companion):** real `proxy_strength` is the out-of-sample AUC of a classifier predicting protected class from the admissible-feature portfolio, with protected class obtained via the BISG / geocoded proxies standard in fair-lending analysis — so the synthetic dose axis has a concrete real-data analog. **But the transfer is structurally asymmetric, and the asymmetry IS the thesis (the sharpest point from round-2 review):** the real companion can carry **C1** (`retained_excess(proxy_strength)` needs only G, which HMDA supplies) but **cannot validate C3-payload's separation curve** — computing `separation_auc` requires ground-truth proxy-vs-legitimate *labels*, which observational real data does not contain. That absence is not a limitation of our experiment; it is C3-floor restated on real ground: the real world is precisely the place where you cannot label a carrier proxy-or-legitimate from data alone. C3 is synthetic-only *of necessity*, and that necessity is the regulatory payload.
- **NOT a defense.** The experiment demonstrates the enumeration/non-identifiability LIMIT. The predictive-vs-proxy judgment is external (per scope above); construction-provenance is relocated to "the documentation layer that makes that substantive judgment auditable," not a technical substitute for it. (If C3-payload finds a reference that separates, *that* is a partial defense and is characterized as such.)
- **NOT a novel statistical technique.** Building `M_compliant` is standard feature-selected ML; the contribution is the joint claim (standard practice + admissible features + correlation structure ⇒ audit-evading disparate impact) + the non-identifiability instantiation. **Literature the paper must engage** (beyond pre-reg scope, flagged so the position isn't naive): Kleinberg / Chouldechova and Hardt-Price-Srebro (fairness-criteria incompatibility, at the audit-instrument layer); Datta-Tschantz-Datta (proxy use / discrimination by association via QII); Slack et al. (adversarial attacks fooling LIME/SHAP — directly relevant to rung 1); the counterfactual/causal-fairness literature (Kusner-Loftus-Russell-Silva; Kilbertus et al.).
