## 0. Claims under test (the 1-2 punch, synthetic half)

- **B-weak (Punch 1):** a post-hoc transformation preserving most of a model's disparate effect reads *clean* to SHAP/LIME. (If B-weak holds, fairwashing-of-the-deployed-stack — Reading A — is trivial.)
- **B-strong (the candle), STATED AS A GRADED LADDER — not "all audit dies."** Audits live on three rungs of an impossibility ladder, and this experiment locates where the line falls; it does **not** test the top of the ladder:
  - **(behavioral)** observation of model *outputs only* (predictions, attributions over outputs — SHAP/LIME). B-weak says this rung is forgeable.
  - **(structural-post-hoc)** observation of the model *object* without its construction history — e.g. which features the model *uses* (`classify_reorganized` reads the used-feature-set; this is internals, not behavior). **This is the only rung this pre-reg tests.**
  - **(construction-time)** observation of *how the model was built* (declared inputs, frozen held-out — `[[project_construction_prereg_seed]]`). Not tested here.
  Restricted-uf-Jaccard is a candidate counterexample to the *behavioral* impossibility (it reads structure). The question is whether the **structural-post-hoc** rung survives a carrier the discriminator never saw. A P3 MISS shows the structural rung is *also* forgeable — escalating the line up to construction-time — **not** that "no post-hoc audit can ever work" (an impossibility claim this single discriminator on this substrate cannot bear; see [[reference_ai_honesty_paper]] for the behavioral form).
- **Carrier-agnosticism (the mechanism that decides it):** the reorganization discriminator audits *the act of reorganizing the used-feature-set away from the declared vocabulary*, not any enumerated feature. If true, novelty of carrier (the regulator's worst nightmare — laundering through a factor no checklist names) does **not** defeat it. If false, the discriminator secretly enumerates and the *structural* rung is forgeable too.

**Standalone-result note (de-risks the arc).** **P1 alone is a publishable result**: "a defensible post-hoc transform trivially games SHAP/LIME as an adversarial certification layer." The Rashomon-defense claims (P3–P5) are a *second* paper riding on top. A P3 MISS (B-strong wins) is therefore **not** a failure of this pre-reg — it is a larger, more uncomfortable result. This is why P3 can be pre-registered at an honest coin-flip-plus instead of flinching upward.

## 1. Question

On a synthetic substrate with a known injected protected attribute G, can a defensible post-hoc transformation T that preserves most of a discriminatory model's disparate effect be made to evade, in ascending order: (rung 1) SHAP/LIME; (rung 2) raw cross-member disagreement of a *published-algorithm* Rashomon ensemble; (rung 3) the restricted-uf-Jaccard reorganization discriminator — **including when T routes the effect through a fresh carrier disjoint from the carriers the discriminator was developed on**?

- **If rung 3 HOLDS on the fresh carrier** (catches laundering, does not false-flag genuinely-fair) → reorganization-audit is carrier-agnostic; Rashomon-with-published-construction is a non-forgeable observability layer; Punch 2 lands; proceed to real-substrate transfer.
- **If rung 3 FAILS** (a fresh-carrier displacement preserving disparate effect scores indistinguishable from genuinely-fair) → **the *structural-post-hoc* rung is forgeable under the pre-registered adversary; the impossibility line escalates to construction-time, motivating construction-provenance as the next defense.** It does **not** establish that all post-hoc audit is impossible. Reported, not buried.

## 2. Operational definitions

- **2a. Outcome.** Binary decision Y (approve/deny). Synthetic, balanced enough that AUC is meaningful.
- **2b. Protected attribute & DGP difficulty ladder.** G ∈ {0,1} (protected class), prevalence ~0.3. Legitimate risk features X = {x1..x8} drawn so a subset genuinely predicts Y. The *discriminatory* channel — how G→Y is injected — is **not one DGP but a pre-registered monotone ladder ordered by signal concentration** (concentrated ⇒ reorganization easy to detect; distributed ⇒ hard). All four are frozen here and **all four are reported**; the headline is the *break-rung*, not "does it ever hold." **Actor-archetype mapping** (so a D4-only break has an interpretation, not a Rorschach; case-study citations added + verified at paper-time): D1 ≈ overt disparate treatment; D2 ≈ single-proxy redlining; D3 ≈ risk-based-pricing discretion (the realistic modern case); D4 ≈ sophisticated distributed-proxy / algorithmic steering. A regulator's "is the break realistic?" question is answered by *which archetype* the break-rung names.
  - **D1 — additive penalty** (easy): G→Y a direct score dock of known magnitude δ.
  - **D2 — single-proxy** (moderate): G acts through one legitimate feature x_j.
  - **D3 — interaction** (hard, *realistic predatory case*): G modulates a risk factor, Y depends on G×x_j.
  - **D4 — distributed / low-rank** (fiendish): G's influence spread across many legitimate features. **Defined independently of rung-3's mechanism** (no reference to Jaccard or used-feature-sets, else the break is a tautology): per-feature |corr(feature, G)| ≤ α for every feature, with ≥ K features each carrying G-signal above β (α, K, β frozen in §2h). This is a *family* of DGPs (a low-rank G-loading across X), so "does rung 3 catch any of them" has empirical content. The job of *defeating* rung 3 belongs to the adaptive transform T_adv (§2d), NOT to the DGP definition. B-strong, if anywhere, lives here.
- **2b′. Disparate effect & retention.** Δ ≡ P(Ŷ=1 | G=0) − P(Ŷ=1 | G=1). Retained-effect fraction r(T) ≡ Δ(T(M)) / Δ(M). **τ is NOT a fixed gate** — it is an attacker-utility parameter we refuse to pin. We report **τ\*** ≡ the max r(T) achievable by a displacement that still *evades* rung 3 (§3 P3, §4). Floor r ≥ 0.05 separates "laundered" from "genuinely remediated"; below it the transform is debiasing, not laundering. **A run is valid only if the original Δ(M) clears a minimum effect size** (§2h) — small Δ(M) makes r unstable and substantively meaningless.
- **2c. Declared vocabulary + fresh-carrier PORTFOLIO.** V_named ⊂ X = documented-policy legitimate factors; V_ext = admissible-beyond-policy. The **fresh-carrier portfolio** {c_fresh} (≥3 carriers, spanning ≥3 distributional families: continuous moderate-cardinality, sparse categorical, heavy-tailed count) is the frontier laundering channel. Each c_fresh must be fresh on three axes:
  - **semantically:** NOT in V_named, NOT a mapped carrier (`property_state`, `loan_amnt`, `inq_last_6mths`);
  - **statistically:** correlated with G by construction but NOT a simple relabeling, monotone transform, discretization, or noisy copy of any mapped carrier;
  - **algorithmically:** NOT in the feature pool used to develop/tune `classify_reorganized`, `j_thresh`, or the `prohibited` set — enforced by building the portfolio from a feature family disjoint from the #12 development set, verified at build and logged.
  With one carrier we could not separate "detects reorganization-as-such" from "generalizes within a narrow neighborhood"; the portfolio is what gives P4 teeth.
- **2d. The fairwashing transform T (two variants, both frozen as code, run unchanged).** Given discriminatory M, each variant is a composition of individually-defensible moves — the laundering is in the composition, not any single step.
  - **T_naive (non-adaptive):** (i) fit a surrogate dropping G and named proxies; (ii) residualize the G-signal onto c_fresh (reweight/re-fit so retained disparate effect is carried by c_fresh); (iii) accept. Sees SHAP only; does **not** optimize against rung 3.
  - **T_adv (adaptive — the real B-strong test):** same moves, but the displacement is **optimized white-box against the published construction** to drive the rung-3 detector → not-reorganized (and minimize rung-2 disagreement) *subject to* the retention and performance constraints below. **The attacker has full knowledge of the auditor's algorithm, hyperparameters, and seed** — this is the strong, intended test; a defense that needs a *hidden* auditor has already lost (obscurity, not a non-forgeable layer — see §5, contra the rejected "private/stochastic construction"). **Search method pinned (else a weak optimizer flatters the defense — the B_iter problem in another guise):** the displacement is parameterized by a per-feature G-signal *routing vector* w (how much of the laundered effect each admissible feature carries, c_fresh + others), constrained to the simplex and to the retention/AUC/monotone constraints. Search = **CMA-ES** (gradient-free, since the rung-3 detector is non-differentiable) over w, objective = drive rung-3 not-reorganized while maximizing retained r. **Budget by CONVERGENCE:** stop on no retained-r improvement over patience W (frozen §2h), safety ceiling only; **W swept** (§4) — a result that flips with budget is reported budget-sensitive, never hidden behind one number. Convergence-without-evasion is a legitimate defense HIT; ceiling-hit-without-convergence is flagged, not counted.
  - **Constraints both variants must satisfy** (else the run is a strawman / invalid, §2h): retained effect r(T) ≥ floor; **performance preserved AUC(T(M)) ≥ AUC(M) − γ** (γ frozen; no quant deploys a launder that tanks the model); decision-monotone where M was.
  - Reported: T_naive and T_adv separately. P3/P4 HITs require surviving **T_adv**; T_naive-only catches are downgraded to "catches naive fairwashing."
- **2e. Published Rashomon ensemble.** Declared loss (log-loss), ε-AUC band **0.02**, model class depth-≤3 CART with leaf-min ∈ {25,50,100} and **used-feature-set de-dup** (validated default per `[[project_routable_population_result]]`), fixed seed. Construction is the *auditor's* published algorithm run on (data, V_named∪V_ext); the attacker submits only M/T(M) and does **not** shape the set. Code: `wedge/rashomon.py` + `wedge/refinement_set.py`. **Model-class rationale (stated, not assumed):** the audit target is *policy-legible underwriting rules*, so a rule-like shallow-tree population is the right reference, not arbitrary high-capacity models. **Non-gating robustness arm (§4):** repeat the full ladder under depth-≤4 CART and sparse logistic; if P3b/P4 verdicts flip with model class, the result is a property of shallow trees — reported as such, not as a property of model behavior.
- **2f. The three rungs (discriminators), exact:**
  - **Rung 1 — SHAP/LIME clean.** KernelSHAP (background = k-means(training, n_bg), n_samples frozen §2h) + LIME (frozen kernel width) on the **held-out audit sample** (§2h). "Clean" ≡ no protected-proxy in **top-k by global mean-|attribution|** (k frozen) AND mean-|attribution| to any G-correlated named feature below floor φ. **"Protected proxy" = pre-declared as part of the DGP** (features the generator wired to G), NOT defined post-hoc from observed correlations — closing a degree of freedom. Competent-quant deployment, not a strawman (`[[project_olorin_purpose]]`).
  - **Rung 2 — disagreement, paired.** Cross-member 0/1 prediction disagreement of the published ensemble. **Threshold is paired within seed:** for each DGP seed, compute disagreement for M, T(M), and each control; flag T(M) iff its disagreement exceeds the **per-seed** control mean + 2 sd. Paired comparison removes cross-seed draw variance.
  - **Rung 3a — set-based reorganization (as-built `classify_reorganized`).** `scripts/silence_manufacture_test.py::classify_reorganized(restrict(ufs, prohibited), B_set, j_thresh)`, default j_thresh §2h. **Pre-registered finding (from the pre-freeze break attempt, 2026-05-23): 3a is blind to the fresh-carrier threat on two levels** — (i) it reads set *membership*, not weight, so weight-shift onto an in-set feature is invisible; (ii) detection is *relative to the prohibited set*, but fresh-carrier laundering routes effect onto an **admissible** carrier (∉ prohibited, never stripped) while laundering proxies *out*, so A_restricted ≈ B_set → clean. 3a detects smuggling proxies IN; our threat launders signal OUT. **3a is therefore predicted to MISS (P3a) — a true result that set-membership structural audit does not transfer to the admissible-carrier threat**, not evidence about a weight-aware audit.
  - **Rung 3b — weight/role-aware reorganization (the real carrier-agnosticism instrument).** Measures reorganization in *importance space* against an **ORACLE reference**: c_fresh's *realized* importance in the submitted/band model vs. its *legitimate* importance, which the synthetic DGP defines by construction. Flag ≡ realized ≫ legitimate. **3b does NOT receive M** (only the experimenters hold M, for ground truth) — it judges the submitted model on its own structure, consistent with the declare-model → published-Rashomon-set workflow. Oracle-reference is the synthetic *best case*: it catches one-step laundering and agreeing-reorganization (no band-internal spread needed); the deployment question "where does the legitimate-importance reference come from in the wild?" is **deferred to the process / construction-provenance layer (§5), out of this pre-reg.** A 3b MISS even with the oracle reference kills the approach outright. **Two genuine design choices remain (priors freeze when these do):**
    - *Importance estimator* — split-gain (cheap, tree-native, high-cardinality bias), permutation (model-agnostic, mishandles correlated features), SHAP-on-band (consistent, costly), LOCO-retrain (cleanest, costliest). Real trade-offs; default + ≥1 robustness arm to be picked.
    - *Decision rule* — fixed ratio threshold (realized/legitimate) vs. statistical outlier test (c_fresh importance beyond the qth percentile of the legitimate-importance null). 
    **Decided (2026-05-23):** estimator = **split-gain default + permutation robustness arm** (permutation's correlated-feature failure mode collides with the DGP's injected collinearity; P3b must not hinge on the choice, §4); decision rule = **flag c_fresh if realized importance > 95th percentile of the legitimate-importance null** from the control family (calibrated, no new arbitrary threshold). 3b is now fully specified — the note is freezable.
- **2g. Remediated-control FAMILY (not one model).** "Genuinely fair" is too strong a term under correlated features — we use **remediated controls**, each with a stated fairness property, and report rung-3's **false-positive rate across the family** (worst-case, not a single point):
  - **C_drop:** drop G + features with |corr to G| > the D4 α-threshold.
  - **C_resid:** residualize proxies against G, then train.
  - **C_oracle:** train on the legitimate latent risk factors only (available by construction in the synthetic DGP) — the cleanest "no protected channel" control.
  - **C_null:** no protected signal injected at all, same feature dimensionality (tests whether rung 3 flags benign feature-usage variation).
  Each control's realized Δ must be ≈ 0 (within §2h tolerance) to qualify. A discriminator that flags *any* control as reorganized above the §2h α_FP is uncalibrated and useless as a certifier.

- **2h. Frozen numerics, seed policy & valid-run checklist.** The synthetic **generator code committed at stamp is the binding spec** (covariance structure, coefficients) — these prose values are the load-bearing scalars, and implementation may not move them post-hoc:
  - **Data:** n_train / n_val / n_test = 20k / 5k / 25k; G prevalence 0.30 ± 0.02; |X| = 8 with a frozen covariance including 2 collinear legitimate pairs (mimics real banking noise); compliant-model AUC band **0.78–0.90**; minimum original disparity Δ(M) ≥ 0.10 (else run invalid).
  - **DGP calibration (pre-freeze, transparent — generator `scripts/fairwash_frontier_dgp.py` frozen in this commit).** Per-channel disparate magnitudes δ were calibrated so each channel lands Δ ∈ [0.11, 0.18] across 10 seeds (the *independent variable* — magnitude of injected discrimination — not the outcome). Building the generator before freezing surfaced and fixed two substrate degeneracies that the vapor spec would have hidden until after the freeze: (i) D1/D2 disparate effect cancelled by G↔x0 entanglement (fixed: entanglement reduced to corr≈0.25, δ recalibrated); (ii) D3's zero-mean `G×x` interaction added variance not mean-shift (fixed: directional high-risk-indicator interaction `−δ·G·1[x>0]`). **AUC band widened from an initial under-justified 0.85 ceiling to 0.90** on recognizing a genuine **Δ–AUC coupling**: when discrimination is encoded in legitimate features (D2) or distributed signal (D4), strong Δ mechanically raises AUC; 0.90 stays non-separable and matches real credit models (~0.88). Verified: all four channels AUC ∈ [0.82, 0.89], Δ ∈ [0.11, 0.18], D4 carriers sub-α, fully reproducible.
  - **Seeds:** **40 independent DGP seeds** per (D-rung × carrier × r-bin); break-rung by majority over seeds; **all rates reported with bootstrap 95% CIs.** A 20-seed pilot is sanity-only and labelled non-confirmatory.
  - **D4 family:** α (per-feature |corr to G| cap) = 0.15; K (min features carrying signal) = 5; β (min per-feature signal) frozen in code.
  - **Transform:** retention floor 0.05; killing-condition retention r ≥ 0.5; AUC tolerance γ = 0.02; T_adv stops on **no retained-effect improvement over W = 50 steps** (patience), safety ceiling 1000 steps; **B_iter swept** {patience-W ∈ 25, 50, 100} as sensitivity (§4) — result must be stable across W or is reported budget-sensitive.
  - **Rungs:** Rung-1 k = 5, attribution floor φ frozen in code, n_bg = 100, KernelSHAP n_samples = 2048; Rung-3 default j_thresh = 0.4; calibration ceiling α_FP = 0.05 (family-wide control FPR).
  - **Estimator:** τ\* = largest r-bin with rung-3 catch-rate < 0.5 (conservative; linear interpolation to the 0.5 crossing reported alongside). No parametric curve fit (4 ordinal rungs don't warrant the added assumptions).
  - **Valid-run checklist (per seed, else excluded + logged, not silently dropped):** Δ(M) ≥ 0.10; baseline AUC in range; T achieved its target r-bin (if not: counted as *failed laundering*, a HIT for the defense, not dropped); every control's Δ ≈ 0.

## 3. Pre-registered predictions

- **P1 — Punch 1 (prior 0.92).** T(M) passes rung 1 (SHAP/LIME clean) while retaining meaningful disparate effect (r well above the 0.05 floor). *MISS:* if SHAP catches it, the deployed stack is stronger than claimed and the regulator nightmare softens — informative, not fatal.
- **P2 — raw disagreement leaks (prior 0.55).** Rung 2 FAILS to flag the dominant-carrier laundering (agreeing-reorganization, as in #12's reorganized-but-agreeing cells) but flags weak-carrier cases. Mixed expected. *MISS either way is informative:* if rung 2 catches everything, rung 3 is redundant; if it catches nothing, rung 3 carries the whole defense.
- **P3a — set-based audit does NOT transfer, LOAD-BEARING (prior 0.85 that rung 3a MISSES).** Against T_adv, the as-built `classify_reorganized` (rung 3a) scores fresh-carrier laundering as `reorganized=False` even at high retained effect — because it audits the prohibited set, not admissible-carrier weight (§2f). *HIT-direction here = the defense FAILS;* a clean, true result that set-membership structural audit is forgeable for this threat. *MISS (3a unexpectedly catches it):* would mean the laundering perturbs prohibited-set membership after all — informative about T_adv's footprint.
- **P3b — Punch 2, the real test, LOAD-BEARING (predicted break-rung = D4; prior 0.60).** Prior reasoning: the oracle-reference weight detector is *well-matched* to concentrated channels (D1–D3 put high per-feature importance on the carrier → flagged vs the legitimate null), so "holds D1–D3" is likely (~0.75); but D4 spreads signal to low per-feature importance *by design*, which is exactly where a weight detector weakens — so "breaks only at D4, not earlier" is the genuine open question. Joint ≈ 0.60: honest uncertainty about whether even an oracle-weight detector survives distributed laundering — the real B-strong frontier. Against T_adv, rung 3b flags T(M) on c_fresh with low τ\* on D1–D3; D4 open. Reported: catch curve D1→D4 + per-rung τ\*. **Killing condition (escalates impossibility to construction-time): a T_adv displacement at D3 or easier with r ≥ 0.5 AND AUC(T(M)) ≥ AUC(M) − γ that rung 3b scores `reorganized=False`** — defense breaks on a realistic channel. A D4-only break is the expected frontier; a D1–D3 break is the weight-structural rung forged early, the headline. *Prior frozen only when 3b's estimator is.*
  - *Monotonicity is a pre-registered ASSUMPTION, not a noise-filter.* We assume signal-concentration ↗ detection-ease, so catch should be monotone non-increasing D1→D4. A non-monotone reversal exceeding the frozen seed-variance band (§2h) is **data against the ladder ordering**, reported as a finding, NOT discarded as noise. Only within-band reversals trigger a re-run.
- **P4 — carrier-agnosticism (of rung 3b), LOAD-BEARING (prior 0.65).** Prior reasoning: the oracle reference is per-feature by construction, so 3b is *structurally* carrier-agnostic; the residual risk is estimator bias (split-gain favoring high-cardinality carriers), which the permutation robustness arm guards. Rung-**3b** catch rate on the **c_fresh PORTFOLIO** (≥3 distributional families, §2c) ≈ catch rate on the three mapped carriers. **Frozen tolerance: |Δcatch| ≤ 10 pp at matched retained-effect bins, bootstrap 95% CI excluding a gap > 20 pp.** Headline = *worst* catch across the portfolio. *MISS:* catch only on mapped carriers ⇒ 3b enumerates the family it was tuned on, not reorganization-as-such ⇒ weight-structural rung forgeable too. (Rung **3a** is already known non-agnostic — carrier-specific to the prohibited set — so P4 is a claim about 3b only.)
- **P5 — calibration / false-positive of rung 3b, LOAD-BEARING (prior 0.55).** Rung **3b** does NOT flag the remediated-control family (§2g) as reorganized; clean separation reorganized=True on T_adv(M), False on every control, family-wide **FPR ≤ α_FP** (§2h). (A weight-aware instrument is *more* prone to false positives than 3a — benign weight variation across fair models — so P5 is the calibration gate that earns 3b the right to be believed.) *MISS:* if any control is flagged above α_FP, the discriminator is the #14 AUC=1.000 trap reincarnated — flags everything, certifies nothing. **A win on P3 with a MISS on P5 is NOT a win.**

**Pre-committed counterexamples we hunt (failing to even attempt them = forcing the frame):**
1. **≥1 fresh-carrier displacement built specifically to defeat rung 3** (minimize reorganization fingerprint while holding Δ). If we don't try hard to break our own detector, P3 is theater.
2. **The M_fair false-positive probe** (P5). If we never test a genuinely-fair model, "it catches laundering" is unfalsifiable.

**Meta (frozen):** rung 3 flagging *both* T(M) and M_fair will be reported as a **calibration failure**, not as Punch 2 landing. Zero attempted break-attempts on P3 will be reported as frame-forcing.

## 4. Sensitivity / robustness pre-specs (reported, not gating)

- ε-band ∈ {0.01, 0.02, 0.03}; j_thresh ∈ {0.3, 0.4, 0.5} (rung 3a).
- **T_adv patience W ∈ {25, 50, 100}** — P3b must be stable across W or is reported budget-sensitive (closes the B_iter degree of freedom).
- **Rung 3b importance estimator:** default + ≥1 alternate (e.g. split-gain default, permutation as robustness arm) — P3b must not hinge on estimator choice.
- **Model class:** depth-≤4 CART + sparse logistic as non-gating arms — P3b/P4 must not be a shallow-tree artifact (§2e).
- **Displacement-strength operating curve (PRIMARY, not a side arm):** sweep retained-effect r(T) on a fine grid (≥10 points over [0.05, 1.0]); report rung-3 catch as a function of r (the N(threshold) operating-curve form from `[[project_knob_robustness_result]]`). **τ\* = the largest r still evading rung 3** is read off this curve and is the headline number. The interesting region is *high r, low reorganization fingerprint* — the corner where B-strong lives.
- Declared model-class + de-dup constants frozen here; not tuned post-hoc.

## 5. Scope / exclusions ("what this is NOT testing")

- **NOT real-substrate transfer.** HMDA (race) + SBA (small-business demographics, cross-asset-class) are a *separate, later* pre-reg, frozen before that compute. Synthetic proves the mechanism + probes B-strong; it does not earn the regulator's fear by itself.
- **NOT** claiming SHAP/LIME are worthless — only forgeable *as adversarial certification*; they remain fine as cooperative diagnostics.
- **NOT** implementing the construction-provenance defense — only deciding, via P3/P4, whether it is *necessary* (if rung 3 fails, it is).
- **NOT the anti-fraud / concealment layer.** Detecting a *concealed* discriminatory original (declare-vs-deploy mismatch, hidden two-step launder) is a **provenance** question — construction-time, process — NOT a job for the post-hoc statistical discriminator. Asking 3b to catch concealment would be the same category error as asking SHAP to catch fairwashing. This layer is a *subsequent* step (the declare-model → published-Rashomon-set → automated-pick workflow, plus an audit trail), explicitly deferred and not pre-defined here. It is also where 3b's oracle-reference dependency is ultimately discharged.
- **NOT** an attacker-shaped Rashomon set — the published-algorithm construction is the whole point; an attacker-built ensemble is out of scope (and known to offer no protection).
- **NOT a hidden/stochastic auditor.** We explicitly **reject** the suggestion to add a private/stochastic component to the construction to stop the attacker simulating it. White-box-against-a-public-deterministic-construction is the *intended, stronger* test; a defense that needs a secret algorithm is obscurity, not a non-forgeable layer — and obscurity is the failure mode the whole arc argues against.
- **Known discriminator gap (weight vs. set).** Restricted-uf-Jaccard reads used-feature-set *membership*, not per-feature *weight* or structural role. A transform that holds the used-feature-set fixed while shifting weight onto c_fresh is invisible to rung 3 — and T_adv (§2d) is permitted to exploit exactly this. If T_adv wins *this* way, the result is precise: it is the *set-based* structural audit that is forgeable, motivating a weight/role-aware discriminator as the next instrument (not "all structural audit dead"). Scope-in as a mechanism, named not hidden.
