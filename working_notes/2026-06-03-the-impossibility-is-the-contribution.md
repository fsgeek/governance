**The sleight that fooled the proposer (me):** "normative not evidentiary" is
TRUE of the trivial part (abstract value judgment = already the law, C3-irrelevant)

frame survives only by silently swapping which one it describes. Every escape has
now been shown to commit some version of this swap.

## The reframe: the impossibility IS the result (the adversary's inverse)

We spent the lineage treating each collapse as a loss — "another escape failed."
That was the error of stance, not of method. Read together, the six collapses are
not six failures; they are **a six-point proof of a positive thesis:**

> **Fair-lending adjudication of admissible-feature-set choice is constitutively
> NORMATIVE, not technical. No quantity from the observable joint (×3), no
> magnitude of the choice's effect (×1), no cryptographic attestation of the
> construction (×1), and no disclosure-schema relocation to a human forum (×1)
> can substitute for a society-level value judgment about which feature-sets are
> admissible — because every one of them collapses to the same unobservable:
> the bank's intent over an unrecorded counterfactual choice-set.**

The impossibility was never the obstacle to the contribution. **The impossibility
IS the contribution.** Six independent routes, three of them outside statistics
entirely (crypto, governance, law), all failing in the identical place, is far
stronger evidence that the floor is a property of the OBJECT than any single
detector result could be. A reviewer cannot dismiss it as "you just didn't find
the right method" — the point is precisely that the method-space, exhausted across
domains, has no member that works.
